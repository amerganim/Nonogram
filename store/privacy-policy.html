Daily Nonogram — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Daily Nonogram is a puzzle game. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no social features. This policy explains the small amount of data involved and who handles it.

What the app stores on your device

Your puzzle progress, your daily streak, which puzzles you have finished, and your settings are saved only on your phone. None of it is uploaded, and none of it is visible to the developer or anyone else.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. You can also clear it at any time from Android Settings → Apps → Daily Nonogram → Storage → Clear data.

What the app sends over the network

The app itself makes no network requests. The puzzles ship inside the app, so it works fully offline. Two Google services do connect out:

Advertising — Google AdMob

Ads are served by Google AdMob. To do that, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier
  • your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it
  • device information such as model, operating system version and language
  • whether an ad was shown, viewed or interacted with

This is used to select and measure ads, and — depending on your settings and region — to personalise them. The developer of this app does not receive any of this data; only aggregate counts of how many ads were shown.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings → Google → Ads. Buying the ad-free option in the app also stops ads being requested at all.

Google's handling of this data is covered by the Google Privacy & Terms.

Purchases — Google Play Billing

If you buy the ad-free upgrade or a hint pack, the purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. Payment details never reach this app and are never seen by the developer. The app only asks Google Play what you own, so it can unlock what you paid for — which is also why your purchase is restored automatically if you reinstall or change phone.

Analytics

This version of the app contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Aggregate crash and performance data may still be reported to Google Play Console by Android itself, as it is for every app on the Play Store; that is anonymous and not something this app collects or can identify you from.

If an analytics or crash-reporting library is added in a future version, this policy and the Play Data Safety declaration must both be updated before that version ships.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and is not enrolled in Google Play's Designed for Families programme. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Stop ad data collection: buy the ad-free option, or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings.
  • Delete everything: uninstall the app, or clear its data.
  • Ask a question: use the contact address below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it, and the updated version will be published at this same address.
